# Repeat unresolved complaints

When a customer has three or more repeat complaints in 90 days, or any formal escalation,
assign a senior complaint owner and arrange a human callback within 24 hours. Resolve the
service issue before discussing a commercial retention offer. The colleague records the outcome.
